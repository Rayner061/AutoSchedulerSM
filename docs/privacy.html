Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Autopost is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish their own content to their own Facebook Page, Instagram account and TikTok account.

What data is used

  • Access tokens issued by Meta and TikTok, used only to publish posts the owner scheduled.
  • Basic account information (account name and ID) to confirm which account to post to.
  • Photos, videos and captions the owner places in their own Google Sheet and Google Drive.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets or in the owner's private Google Sheet. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

To delete all data, remove the app's access in your Facebook, Instagram or TikTok settings, and delete the repository secrets and the Google Sheet. Questions can be sent to the repository owner through GitHub.
