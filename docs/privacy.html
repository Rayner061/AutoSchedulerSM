Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Autopost is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish their own content to their own Facebook Page, Instagram account, TikTok account and YouTube channel.

What data is used

  • Access tokens issued by Meta, TikTok and Google (YouTube), used only to publish posts the owner scheduled.
  • Basic account information (account name and ID) to confirm which account to post to.
  • Photos, videos and captions the owner places in their own Google Sheet and Google Drive.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets or in the owner's private Google Sheet. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube

Autopost uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's channel. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. Autopost's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion

To delete all data, remove the app's access in your Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or Google account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions), and delete the repository secrets and the Google Sheet. Questions can be sent to the repository owner through GitHub.
