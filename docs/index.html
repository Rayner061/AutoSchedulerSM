AutoSchedulerSM

A private, single-user tool that publishes the owner's scheduled posts to the owner's own Facebook Page, Instagram account, TikTok account and YouTube channel.

YouTube This app uses YouTube API Services

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Connect YouTube Connect TikTok

How it works

  1. The owner lists posts (date, caption, video or photo) in their own private Google Sheet.
  2. A scheduled job checks the sheet and, at the scheduled time, uploads each post to the owner's own accounts using the official platform APIs (for YouTube: the YouTube Data API).
  3. The result (a link to the published post) is written back to the sheet.
